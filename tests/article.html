Why sleep helps us learn

Learning doesn’t end when you close your textbook. While you sleep, your brain continues to work with the things you encountered during the day.

Researchers call this process memory consolidation. New information becomes more stable, and useful connections can emerge between ideas that first seemed unrelated.

Give an idea some space

Spacing your study sessions gives your brain more opportunities to revisit an idea. Instead of repeating a fact ten times in one sitting, return to it over several days.

A short rest can be part of learning, too.

  • Try recalling the main idea before checking your notes.

  • Explain it aloud in your own words.
